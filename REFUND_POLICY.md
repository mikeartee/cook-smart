## BETA Period

During BETA testing, Cook Smart is **FREE**. No refunds applicable.

## Post-BETA Refunds

### Subscription Refunds
- **Weekly/Monthly**: Refunds processed through Stripe within 7 days of purchase
- **Yearly**: Refunds available within 30 days of purchase
- **BETA Pre-Purchase**: Refunds available until full app launch

### Refund Process
1. Email: services.cooksmart@gmail.com
2. Include: Order ID, email address, reason for refund
3. Response time: Within 24 hours (business hours)
4. Processing: 5-10 business days after approval
5. Business hours: Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 5 PM Central Time

### Non-Refundable
- Partial subscription periods after use
- Purchases made with promotional pricing (except BETA pre-purchase)
- Refunds requested after stated periods
- Subscriptions canceled during grace period (no charges made during grace period)

### Failed Payment Grace Period
- No refunds applicable during 7-day grace period
- No charges are made during grace period
- Update payment method to continue service
- If payment succeeds after grace period, standard refund policy applies

### Automatic Renewal Refunds
- Refunds for automatic renewals follow the same policy as initial purchases
- Cancel before renewal date to avoid charges
- No refunds for renewals after the refund period expires

### Technical Issues
Refunds may be provided for technical issues preventing app use, evaluated case-by-case.

### Payment Processor
All refunds processed through Stripe according to their terms and applicable law.
