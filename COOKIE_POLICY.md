**Effective Date**: [To be set at launch]

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you use Cook Smart.

## 2. Types of Cookies

### 2.1 Essential Cookies
Required for App functionality (cannot be disabled):
- Authentication tokens
- Session management
- Security features
- User preferences

### 2.2 Performance Cookies
Help us understand App usage:
- Page load times
- Error tracking
- Feature usage analytics
- Performance metrics

### 2.3 Functional Cookies
Remember your preferences:
- Dietary restrictions
- Language preferences
- Display settings
- Saved filters

## 3. Cookies We Use

| Cookie | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|------|---------|----------|
| auth_token | Essential | Authentication | Session |
| session_id | Essential | Session management | Session |
| user_prefs | Functional | Preferences | 1 year |
| analytics_id | Performance | Analytics | 1 year |

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services may set cookies:
- Spoonacular (recipes)
- Edamam (recipes)
- Open Food Facts (barcode)
- Stripe (payments)

## 5. Managing Cookies

### Browser Settings
- Chrome: Settings > Privacy > Cookies
- Firefox: Settings > Privacy > Cookies
- Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
- Edge: Settings > Privacy > Cookies

### Mobile
- iOS: Settings > Safari > Block Cookies
- Android: Settings > Site Settings > Cookies

Disabling essential cookies will prevent App use.

## 6. Local Storage

We also use browser local storage to:
- Cache ingredient data
- Store user inventory
- Improve performance
- Enable offline functionality

## 7. GDPR Compliance

EU users will see a cookie consent banner for non-essential cookies. You can:
- Know what cookies we use
- Consent to non-essential cookies
- Withdraw consent anytime
- Access and delete your data
