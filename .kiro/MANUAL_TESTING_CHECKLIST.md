## 🐛 If You Find Issues:

**For each issue, note:**
1. What screen you were on
2. What you did (tapped X button, entered Y text, etc.)
3. What happened (crashed, wrong color, button didn't work)
4. What you expected to happen

**Example:**
- Screen: Add Ingredient
- Action: Tapped "Save" button
- Result: Nothing happened
- Expected: Ingredient should be saved and appear in list
