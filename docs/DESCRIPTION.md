# Cook Smart - Project Description

## Overview
Cook Smart is a comprehensive recipe generation application that transforms how users discover and create meals based on their available ingredients. The app combines intelligent inventory management with advanced recipe matching algorithms to provide personalized cooking solutions.

## Core Functionality
- **Smart Ingredient Inventory**: Users can build and maintain their ingredient inventory through manual selection, barcode scanning, or custom entries
- **Intelligent Recipe Matching**: Advanced algorithms provide both exact matches and near-matches with missing ingredient highlighting
- **Dietary Accommodation**: Comprehensive support for dietary restrictions and food allergies with customizable filtering
- **Shopping Integration**: Seamless shopping list generation for missing ingredients
- **Social Features**: Referral system with rewards and gamified point accumulation

## Target Market
- Home cooks seeking meal inspiration from available ingredients
- Budget-conscious individuals wanting to minimize food waste
- People with dietary restrictions needing specialized recipe filtering
- Busy professionals looking for quick meal solutions

## Business Model
- Free BETA phase for initial 250+ users
- Post-BETA subscription tiers: Weekly ($2.99), Monthly ($6.99), Yearly ($34.99)
- BETA pre-purchase discount: $24.99 yearly (30% off)
- Viral referral system with unlimited earning potential

## Technical Innovation
- Multi-API recipe integration for maximum reliability
- Real-time barcode scanning with food database integration
- Cross-platform development for seamless mobile conversion
- AWS-powered scalable infrastructure
- Discord integration for community feedback and monitoring

## Development Status
Currently in Phase 1 (Foundation & Infrastructure) with comprehensive planning complete and automated quality assurance systems implemented.
