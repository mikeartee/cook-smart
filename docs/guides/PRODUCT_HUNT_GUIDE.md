# Product Hunt - Complete Guide

## What is Product Hunt?

**Product Hunt** (producthunt.com) is the #1 platform for discovering new products, especially tech/apps.

**Think of it as:**
- Reddit for new products
- Where tech enthusiasts discover apps
- Free marketing to 5+ million users
- Potential to get 500-5,000 signups in 24 hours

## How It Works

### Daily Ranking System:
- New products launch every day
- Users upvote products they like
- Top products get featured on homepage
- Runs from 12:01 AM - 11:59 PM PST

### Typical Results:
- **Top 5 products:** 1,000-5,000 signups
- **Top 10 products:** 500-2,000 signups
- **Top 20 products:** 200-1,000 signups
- **Below top 20:** 50-500 signups

## Launch Strategy

### Preparation (2 hours):

**1. Create Account** (5 min)
- Go to producthunt.com
- Sign up (use your personal account)
- Complete profile with photo

**2. Write Description** (30 min)
```
Title: Cook Smart - Stop Wasting Food, Start Saving Money

Tagline: Scan ingredients, get recipes, reduce waste

Description:
Ever open your fridge and have no idea what to cook? Or throw away 
expired food you forgot about?

Cook Smart solves both problems:
📦 Track ingredients with barcode scanning
🍽️ Get recipe suggestions from what you have
⏰ Expiry alerts so nothing goes to waste
💰 Save $200+/month on groceries

Perfect for:
- Busy parents
- Budget-conscious cooks
- Anyone tired of wasting food

🎁 FREE during beta (normally $34.99/year)
🎯 Join 250+ beta testers

Built with React Native, Node.js, and love ❤️
```

**3. Prepare Media** (1 hour)
- **Thumbnail:** App icon (512x512)
- **Gallery Images:** 4-6 screenshots showing:
  1. Barcode scanning in action
  2. Recipe suggestions
  3. Ingredient inventory
  4. Shopping list
- **Video (optional but recommended):** 30-60 sec demo

**4. Choose Launch Day** (5 min)
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Avoid:** Monday (too busy), Friday (low traffic), weekends
- **Best time:** Launch at 12:01 AM PST to maximize voting time

### Launch Day (Active participation required):

**Hour 1-3 (Critical):**
- Respond to EVERY comment within 5 minutes
- Thank people for upvotes
- Answer questions
- Be genuine and helpful

**Hour 4-8:**
- Keep responding to comments
- Share on your social media
- Ask friends/family to upvote (but don't spam)

**Hour 9-24:**
- Continue engagement
- Monitor ranking
- Celebrate if you hit top 5!

## Success Tips

### DO:
✅ Launch on Tuesday/Wednesday
✅ Respond to every comment quickly
✅ Be authentic and humble
✅ Share your story (why you built it)
✅ Offer exclusive deal for Product Hunt users
✅ Have a working product (you do!)
✅ Prepare for traffic spike (your AWS can handle it)

### DON'T:
❌ Ask for upvotes directly (against rules)
❌ Use fake accounts (you'll get banned)
❌ Launch on Monday or weekend
❌ Ignore comments
❌ Be salesy or pushy
❌ Launch if product is broken
❌ Forget to test your website/app first

## Questions?

Common questions answered:

**Q: Do I need to pay?**
A: No, it's 100% free.

**Q: What if I don't get many upvotes?**
A: You still get exposure and feedback. Even "failed" launches get 50-100 signups.

**Q: Can I launch multiple times?**
A: No, one launch per product. Make it count.

**Q: What if my app crashes?**
A: Have a backup plan. Your AWS setup should handle it, but monitor closely.

**Q: Should I hire a "Product Hunt hunter"?**
A: No, authentic launches from founders perform better.
