# Community Guidelines - Cook Smart

**Effective Date**: [To be set at launch]  
**Last Updated**: November 13, 2025

## 1. Our Community

Cook Smart is a community of food enthusiasts helping each other reduce waste and discover new recipes. These guidelines ensure a positive experience for everyone.

## 2. Core Values

- **Respect**: Treat others with kindness and consideration
- **Inclusivity**: Welcome users of all backgrounds and skill levels
- **Helpfulness**: Share knowledge and support fellow users
- **Honesty**: Provide accurate information and genuine feedback
- **Safety**: Maintain a safe environment for all users

## 3. Expected Behavior

### 3.1 Be Respectful
- Use polite and constructive language
- Respect different cooking styles and preferences
- Accept that people have different dietary needs
- Disagree respectfully without personal attacks

### 3.2 Be Helpful
- Share useful tips and recipe suggestions
- Provide constructive feedback
- Help new users learn the App
- Report bugs and issues appropriately

### 3.3 Be Honest
- Provide accurate feedback during BETA
- Don't manipulate points or referral systems
- Report genuine issues, not false claims
- Be truthful in your profile information

### 3.4 Be Safe
- Don't share personal information publicly
- Report suspicious activity
- Protect your account credentials
- Follow food safety guidelines

## 4. Prohibited Behavior

### 4.1 Harassment
- Bullying or intimidating others
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Sexual harassment
- Threats or violence

### 4.2 Inappropriate Content
- Offensive or explicit material
- Spam or promotional content
- Misleading information
- Content that violates others' rights

### 4.3 Abuse
- Gaming the points system
- Creating fake accounts
- Manipulating referrals
- Exploiting bugs for personal gain

### 4.4 Illegal Activity
- Sharing illegal content
- Promoting dangerous activities
- Violating intellectual property
- Any unlawful behavior

## 5. Content Guidelines

### 5.1 Feedback
- Be specific and constructive
- Focus on the issue, not the person
- Provide examples when possible
- Suggest improvements

### 5.2 Recipe Sharing
- Only share recipes you have rights to
- Give credit to original sources
- Ensure recipes are safe to follow
- Include relevant warnings (allergens, etc.)

### 5.3 Custom Ingredients
- Use accurate names
- Don't create duplicate entries
- Include relevant information
- Don't add inappropriate items

## 6. Reporting Issues

### 6.1 How to Report
Report violations to: tootallgames2020@gmail.com

**Include**:
- Description of the issue
- User involved (if applicable)
- Screenshots or evidence
- Date and time

### 6.2 What to Report
- Harassment or abuse
- Inappropriate content
- System abuse
- Security concerns
- Bugs or technical issues

### 6.3 False Reports
Submitting false reports may result in account action.

## 7. Consequences

### 7.1 Warning
First-time minor violations may receive a warning.

### 7.2 Temporary Suspension
Repeated or moderate violations may result in temporary suspension.

### 7.3 Permanent Ban
Serious violations result in permanent account termination.

### 7.4 Legal Action
Illegal activity may be reported to authorities.

## 8. BETA Participation

### 8.1 Testing Expectations
- Test features thoroughly
- Report bugs promptly
- Provide honest feedback
- Be patient with issues

### 8.2 Feedback Quality
- Be specific about problems
- Include steps to reproduce bugs
- Suggest improvements
- Note what works well

## 9. Privacy and Safety

### 9.1 Personal Information
- Don't share sensitive personal data
- Protect your account credentials
- Be cautious with shared content
- Review privacy settings

### 9.2 Food Safety
- Follow safe food handling practices
- Check for allergens in recipes
- Verify ingredient safety
- Use common sense with cooking

## 10. Moderation

### 10.1 Our Role
We reserve the right to:
- Remove inappropriate content
- Suspend or terminate accounts
- Modify or delete user submissions
- Take action on violations

### 10.2 Appeals
If you believe action was taken in error, contact: tootallgames2020@gmail.com

We may update these guidelines. Continued use constitutes acceptance of changes.
