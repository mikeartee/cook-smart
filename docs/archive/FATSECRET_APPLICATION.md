## Description of Application/Service

Cook Smart is a mobile cooking assistant application designed to help users:

- **Manage ingredient inventory** with barcode scanning
- **Discover recipes** based on available ingredients
- **Track nutritional information** for meals and recipes
- **Plan meals** with dietary restrictions and preferences
- **Generate shopping lists** automatically
- **Monitor dietary goals** and nutritional intake

**FatSecret API Usage**:
We will use the FatSecret Platform API to provide comprehensive nutritional data for:
- Barcode scanning to identify food products and their nutritional content
- Allergen and dietary preference information (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.)
- Complete nutritional search for ingredients and recipes
- Food categories and auto-complete search functionality
- Food images for visual recipe presentation

**Attribution**: We will provide proper attribution to FatSecret as required in all areas where nutritional data is displayed.
